Before you read a chart

  • Every magnitude level is an AI judgment, audited against AI references and not validated by people.
  • The collection has not been proven to hold every AWS announcement.
  • Trends in levels 4 and 5 are provisional: the audits found too few of them to measure.
  • Labels for 2010–2020 come from a later reading protocol that rates higher. On 400 earlier records read again under it, 20.8% were level 3, against 14.0% as recorded. Compare level shares across the pre-COVID, COVID and post-COVID periods with that in mind.
  • Most announcements before 2015 have no service tag, so one service's counts cannot be compared across periods.
  • The final year stops at the collection cutoff, 28 October 2026, so 2026 and its last month are partial.

Search matches announcement titles and our classification reasons. Announcement bodies are not in the public dataset and are not searched.

How big were the changes?

Each announcement is rated on a five-level scale for the size of the product change it announces, from a parameter change (1) to a new product (5). Each bar counts the announcements at that level from 1 January to 28 October of its year. Select a number to list its announcements.
