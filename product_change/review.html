  • Independent agreement measures consistency, not proven accuracy.

  • One initial source reader per record; targeted adjudication does not establish error-free classification.
  • The source review does not extend product-change ratings to 2021–2024 or independently establish completeness of AWS source collection.
  • Each announcement receives its highest substantive product-change level; preview and GA are assessed for the change announced and are not deduplicated into unique product launches.
