Enhancement magnitude audit

Magnitude describes the largest substantiated change for an affected workload. A local adjustment (1) changes a parameter, compatibility, capacity or speed within a workflow. A workflow change (2) adds, removes or automates a step. An architecture change (3) removes a necessary component or enables a different deployment, recovery or data path. U means the source gives insufficient evidence. Customer adoption and business value are outside this measure.

The database includes 3,331 announcements whose primary release type is enhancement, January 2025–October 28, 2026. The charts compare January 1–October 28 in each year. Each announcement counts once. Scope types used in the matrix describe what changed. Magnitude describes the consequence.

Independent readings

Two agents independently read all 48 pilot bodies, sampling eight per group across service limits, performance, integration, automation, configuration and other enhancements. They agreed on 48/48. Three agents then split the full cohort into batches. They used titles and source passages, expanding the reading for architecture decisions. Batch notes can quote or condense source passages. The pilot's double readings take precedence over the batch ratings.

A fresh sample of 80 non-pilot URLs was selected by seeded hash. An independent source reading agreed with batch ratings on 61/80 before reconciliation. The matrix below has first-pass ratings down the side and independent readings across the top. A batch reviewer reconciled disagreements using the full AWS source. We also re-read 39 lower-rated, non-sample records that explicitly mentioned avoiding or replacing components. This changed 20 ratings. Other possible architecture changes remain subject to single-reader uncertainty. These are comparisons between agent judgments. Small changes in category share may reflect rating differences.
