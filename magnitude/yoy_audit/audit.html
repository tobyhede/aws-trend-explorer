Sampling and estimation

We split the matched period in each year into four original magnitude ratings. A seeded hash of each official announcement URL selected 40 single-reader rows from each local, workflow and architecture group. We sampled every single-reader unrated enhancement. This produced 257 fresh cases. The 147 previously reviewed pilot, validation and targeted cases were carried at their existing decisions; they supplied no evidence about unreviewed rows.

Two independent agents saw the title and source text with a random identifier. Year and original labels were omitted from the assignment fields; AWS text could itself mention a date. Readers agreed on 254/257. A third reader reconciled disagreements against the full text while blinded to the original labels. A positive judgment identifies a component made unnecessary or a materially different deployment, recovery or data path enabled by this announcement.

For each year and original rating, the point estimate multiplies the eligible population by the fraction of fresh readings judged architectural. These estimates are added to previously reviewed architecture counts. The 95% model-based ranges use 60,000 seeded draws. For each stratum a Jeffreys Beta(½, ½) prior on the architecture fraction is updated with the sample, then a beta-binomial draw supplies the number of architecture cases among only the unsampled records. We retain observed sample outcomes, and fully examined strata have no sampling uncertainty. Differences are calculated within each paired draw. The ranges are conditional on the source inventory, earlier review decisions and reconciled judgments.

As a sensitivity check, inversion of exact hypergeometric tails with a simultaneous Bonferroni correction across the six non-census strata gives a conservative 95% design-based range for the change in share of -22.2 to +33.7 percentage points.
